# ==== comments ====
cmt 0x10000 Entry: init_system, init_game, title_screen, init_video; then infinite main loop of per-frame subsystems.
cmt 0x12014 update_objects: per-object physics + AI (flap/gravity/direction, home toward players) — the buzzard/enemy movement.
cmt 0x140fe joust_bounce: the core mechanic — on collision the higher rider wins; flips the loser's velocity/direction.
cmt 0x11dd6 control_player: apply joystick (flap/left/right) to a player object; from attract, fire starts the game.
cmt 0x11f28 player_death: decrement death timer, remove player, trigger GAME OVER or respawn message.
cmt 0x146f6 lava_troll: from wave 4+, the lava hand reaches up to grab objects lingering over the pit.
cmt 0x14ada spawn_wave: enemy/egg spawn scheduler; ramps difficulty by halving spawn_interval.
cmt 0x1783c wave_manager: game-phase/round transitions; spawns wave-announcement messages; next-wave setup.
cmt 0x13842 collision_check: broad-phase box test each object vs platforms/objects (test_overlap), then pixel_collision.
cmt 0x14172 add_score_p1: BCD add to player 1 score with carry; awards bonus_life at thresholds; redraws score.
